Beijing Tongren Hospital

Beijing Tongren Hospital, affiliated with Capital Medical University, is a renowned tertiary hospital with specialties in ophthalmology, otorhinolaryngology, and allergy treatment. Established in 1886, it has emerged as a leader in eye care, ear-nose-throat treatments, and allergy management. With over a century of development, Tongren Hospital has garnered national recognition for its advanced medical technologies, including foot and ankle surgery, comprehensive diabetes care, and minimally invasive surgical techniques. The hospital, with over 3,600 staff, serves more than 2.9 million outpatients annually, with 10.9 thousand discharges and 8.1 thousand surgeries performed. It houses key research institutes, a network of academic scholars, and serves as a hub for medical education and international collaborations. Committed to excellence, Tongren Hospital strives to provide top-tier medical services, aiming to become a premier academic medical institution both domestically and internationally, driving innovation and development in the field of medicine.
