About Us

BIOOCUS Medical Group

BIOOCUS Medical Group is committed to advancing the research and development of cellular immunotherapies, offering innovative solutions for refractory diseases. As a leading medical group in China, we integrate the entire value chain with a cutting-edge research center, GMP-certified facilities, a CDMO platform, and comprehensive technology transfer capabilities.

With multiple CGT centers across the country, we provide specialized, high-quality treatment solutions to patients. We collaborate with top universities, research institutes, and over 20 leading hospitals worldwide to deliver transformative therapies.

Our mission is to expand access to breakthrough gene and cellular therapies, ensuring that patients receive the most advanced and effective life-saving treatments available.
